The zone: a Black Flame disaster site radiates out from a dead epicenter. The outer Rim is mild, wireless works fine, and Dat Crystal is scattered in small, common deposits. Push into the violet Dense Field and deposits get rarer but come in big clusters worth securing. Push further into the red Flame Core and both the crystal and the monsters get a lot more serious still.
Tether: beyond a short wireless range around the Bastion (or a built Relay), squads/harvesters/builders can only take orders through a physical cable that pays out behind them as they walk. Monsters that get close to a cable chew through it — a severed unit goes dark (no orders, autonomous retreat toward the nearest anchor) until it reconnects. Cable only runs so far (400 units) — at the end of it a unit just stops; build a Relay further out to extend reach. The Bastion itself is the anchor, so driving it toward a deployed cable reels the slack back in and frees up range. A Relay needs the same live tether back to the network to work at all — chain them (each can be built within range of the Bastion or of an already-finished Relay) and if one in the chain gets severed, everything past it goes dark too until the line is restored.
Drilling: park the Bastion on a found vein's marker and press L. There's no fixed prep phase — throttle the drill (Slow/Normal/Fast, shown once locked in) to trade speed against how much noise it makes. The drill also burns Dat Crystal per second to run, faster at higher throttle — run dry and it stalls until more comes in, so keep harvesters feeding it. Draining a vein pays Condensed Crystal too, more the faster you finished it, so speed pays for itself twice over. Draining the marked Flame Core vein wins the run.
The Bastion fires on nearby enemies while mobile, but goes quiet the moment it locks down to drill.
Harvesters carry no weapons. Left alone they loop automatically: find the nearest Dat Crystal deposit, haul it home, repeat. A manual order pauses that loop until you select them and press Resume Auto-Gather (or R).
Builders place but don't fight: a wall/turret/relay order only queues a ghost site until a builder walks over and finishes it (wall 1s, turret 5s, relay 3s). Walls and turrets need the Bastion locked down drilling; Relays can be queued any time, even while the Bastion is still mobile, so you can push a chain of them ahead of you as you scout. Toggle repair mode (button or G) and they'll auto-seek damaged structures and mend them for a trickle of Dat Crystal/sec instead — including a Relay whose tether got severed: a repair-mode builder will walk up and splice it back in (a few seconds, no Dat cost) even if the Relay itself is at full HP, since the cable and the structure are separate things to break. Idle builders dock at the Bastion.
