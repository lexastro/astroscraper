LANCE CRATER WALLS // heaved ground, not grey spikes

Where the Lance Graft sets down, the ground round it should look like it was broken by the landing, in the same language as the nest breaches: plates of the ground itself, in the ground's own colour, tipped up on edge and lit from the top left, dark struck-glass cracks between them, and a molten middle that cools. The ring still has to read as a wall your units can stand behind.

Each scene loops: heave (the plates kick up), hot (seams glowing), cooled, then hit (enemies chew on the north-east side: plates crack, sag and break away).

Exploration sheet only. The crater is drawn at the game's size (ring radius 78) at 1.3× zoom.
