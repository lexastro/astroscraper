LANCE CRATER WALLS // heaved ground, along the trench and round the crater

Where the Lance Graft sets down, the ground round it should look like it was broken by the landing, in the same language as the nest breaches: plates of the ground itself, in the ground's own colour, tipped up on edge and lit from the top left, dark struck-glass cracks between them, and a molten middle that cools. The ring still has to read as a wall your units can stand behind. Walls now also line both banks of the trench, and the ring is open where the trench comes in, so the trench is a walled road into the crater.

Each scene loops: heave (the plates kick up), hot (seams glowing), cooled, then hit (enemies chew on the crater's north-east side and the far end of the trench's north bank: plates crack, sag and break away).

Exploration sheet only. The crater is drawn at the game's size (ring radius 78) at 1.08× zoom.
