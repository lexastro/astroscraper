Corrupted Units

Each of your base units, taken over by the force behind the Echoes, nests and glass: concept art drawn with the game's own unit code. The original is on the left on Rim ground, the corrupted copy on the right on Core ground, both running the same idle and attack loop. Below each pair: the same two at in-game size (zoom 1× and 2×), then three of each facing off at 1.5×.

Dense glassCrimson spires, shaded like the nest spires, grow through armour, out of muzzles and from pod cells.
Core slabsBlack plates with crimson edges replace a limb or a shield. Glowing cracks run across what's left of the hull.
Echo glitchThe body runs out of sync with itself: red and cyan copies jitter either side, and bands slip sideways or blink out.
Red coreEyes, visors, lamps and the pack ring burn crimson, now and then flickering cyan.
Drained paletteAlly blue goes to slate and white gear to bone. Role colours are kept but scorched, so each unit still reads as what it was.
Attack loop: ~4 s. Facing up in the large view.

The corrupted drawing functions (drawCorruptTrooper, drawCorruptPaladin, drawCorruptGrenadier, drawCorruptMarksman, drawCorruptRocketeer, drawCorruptMech, plus the dispatchers drawCorruptBody and drawCorruptFx) sit in one block in this file's script, marked CORRUPTED UNIT ART (paste-ready). They use only the game's own unit primitives (rP, rO, rLimb, palState…) and the same unit fields, so they can be pasted into datascraper.html next to the existing unit art. None of this is wired into gameplay.
