Controls
Left-drag: select units · Click Bastion: select it & open its build menu · Click the Squads/Harvesters count: select all of that type
Right-click: squads move/attack · harvesters move/harvest a node/return to drop off at the Bastion
WASD or edge-drag (middle mouse) pans · scroll zooms
1 wall · 2 turret · L lock down / skip prep · R resume harvester auto-gather · Esc cancel
Loop: drive the Bastion into a purple zone and scout it to reveal the green mining-point marker. Park the Bastion on it and press L to lock down — you get 45s to fortify before waves hit, then mining runs until the hold timer completes. Press L again during prep to skip straight to the waves. Walls/turrets only work while locked down, and never on bare rock.
Terrain: rock blocks movement/building and enemies too; mud slows everyone but can still be crossed.
The Bastion fires on nearby enemies while mobile, but goes quiet the moment it locks down.
Harvesters carry no weapons. Left alone they loop automatically: find the nearest resource, haul it home, repeat. Giving one a manual order pauses that loop until you select it and press Resume Auto-Gather (or R). Build more from the Bastion's build menu.
Squads can no longer gather resources — that's the harvesters' job now.
` toggles debug: reveal the whole map and all enemies.
