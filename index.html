Build at Bastion
Goal: harvesters gather Dat Crystal automatically. Scout a vein (dashed circle), park the Bastion on its marker, press L to drill — the drill spends 60s warming up (no burn, no waves yet) before it actually starts. Drain veins for rewards — drain 3 and the hidden Living Crystal vein reveals itself; drain that one to win.
Controls: drag to select · right-click to move/attack/harvest · WASD or middle-drag to pan, scroll to zoom
Touch: tap to select · tap empty ground/an enemy/a resource to move/attack/harvest · drag to pan · hold still then drag to box-select · pinch to zoom · double-tap to select all of a kind
Build: 1 wall · 2 turret · 3 relay · 4 depot (drop-off closer than the Bastion) — walls/turrets need the Bastion locked down drilling, relays/depots can go up any time
Tether: wireless works freely on the Rim; past it, units need a live cable back to the Bastion or a Relay, or they go dark — chain Relays to push your reach further out. A dark unit still takes orders, but its Sanity drains; reconnect in time to recover it, or it defects into a hostile Corrupted enemy
N ping (reveals far-off Dat Crystal, 60s cooldown) · V follow Bastion (escorts it as it moves, until given another order) · G repair mode · R resume harvester auto-gather · Esc cancel, or pause the whole game if nothing's open · ` reveal map (debug)
