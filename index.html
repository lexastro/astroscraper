Goal: harvesters gather Dat Crystal automatically. The ground is cracked all over: follow the pulses of energy running through the cracks back to their source to find a vein, then park the Bastion on its glowing centre and press L to lock on. Veins emerge, ripen a tier at a time, then go unstable and collapse — and new ones emerge elsewhere. Glowing stray pockets give a bearing toward the nearest vein when a unit reaches one; two bearings from different angles triangulate it. Locking on starts the strike at once: press Space to stop the needle on a good band of the strip (the thin sliver is a tier above the vein; red ruptures it) — the meter zooms in on where you hit and the drill starts; the first wave arrives about 20s later. While it drills, the needle drifts; Stabilizers hold it steady, and the tier you're paid is where it spends most of its time. If it drifts into the red, the vein ruptures and damages the Bastion. Drain veins for rewards — after 3, the contract vein stirs: it gives off no pulses, so triangulate it with bearings from stray pockets, then drain it to win.
Controls: drag to select · right-click to move/attack/harvest · right-click a friendly unit or building with combat units selected to guard it (escort it, fight off anything that comes near, then fall back) ·WASD or middle-drag to pan, scroll to zoom
Touch: tap to select · tap empty ground/an enemy/a resource to move/attack/harvest · drag to pan · hold still then drag to box-select · pinch to zoom · double-tap to select all of a kind
Build: 1 wall · 2 turret · 3 relay · 4 depot (drop-off closer than the Bastion) · 5 fire a Stabilizer from the Bastion (3 charges, one back every 15s; aim at a vein's drifting hotspot and lead it — a hit pins it, buys the vein 15s before it collapses, widens the strike bands and steadies the needle) — everything can be built any time within range · right-click a still-queued structure to cancel it for a full refund
Tether: wireless works freely on the Rim; past it, units need a live cable back to the Bastion or a Relay, or they go dark — chain Relays to push your reach further out. A dark unit still takes orders, but its Sanity drains; reconnect in time to recover it, or it defects into a hostile Corrupted enemy
N ping, then click a spot within the dashed ring to scan it (permanently finds deposits and vein mining points there, shows enemies there for 20s; 60s cooldown) · V follow Bastion (escorts it as it moves, until given another order) · G repair mode · R resume harvester auto-gather · Esc cancel, or pause the whole game if nothing's open · ` reveal map (debug)
